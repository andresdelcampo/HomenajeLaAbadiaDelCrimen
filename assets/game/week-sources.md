# Seven-day explorer: evidence and scope

Route expansion entry point: [`../../ROUTE_HANDOFF.md`](../../ROUTE_HANDOFF.md). Reviewed traces now live in `tools/routes/*.json` and are compiled with `tools/build-week-routes.py`. The explorer accepts any character/phase and separate floor segments. Only the 201-point welcome route is currently reviewed. The site retains a Printed map / Reconstructed map selector; mixed-floor print mode uses reconstructed upper-floor insets rather than guessing a registration for the printed upper plans. See the handoff for the complete workflow and limits.

This is an editorial reading of the reconstructed VigasocoSDL game logic, not a replay or an emulator. It does not claim that all versions behave identically. Spanish and English dialogue is exported verbatim from that port's phrase tables; the English text is not presented as a verified extraction of the English DOS disk. Spelling in quotations is intentionally preserved.

Local source base: `Fuentes/VigasocoSDL-master/VigasocoSDL-master/core/abadia/`.

Day I Nona route trial (2026-09-13): the printed MicroHobby/Retro Gamer map is aligned to the fixed game-coordinate route with the user-selected 2.5° clockwise rotation, 108% scale, −4 source-image pixels horizontally and 0 vertically, about (350, 500) on the 1323 × 982 image. The route retains the original uniform world-to-display mapping. Other portraits keep approximate printed-map anchors transformed with the artwork. Only this phase uses the calibration; the other 40 phases retain their existing map and schematic movements. The SVG height-grid plans remain available for comparison in `tools/map-overlay.html`. The printed architecture and collision contours are different representations, so minor local discrepancies remain.

`tools/trace-abbot-welcome.py` compiles a small headless harness from the preserved `BuscadorRutas`, `RejillaPantalla`, `FijarOrientacion` and `PersonajeConIA::avanzaPosicion` sources. It runs the original room search, local 24-by-24 height-grid search, path reconstruction and stair advancement. Instead of rewinding the character after generating movement commands, the harness records and retains the positions visited during reconstruction. Graphics, phrase timing, actor collisions and door leaves are omitted. Thus this is an unobstructed reconstruction between scripted destinations, not a recorded playthrough: the Abbot's approach to William during the first phrase and detours around actors can vary.

The two legs run from `(88h,84h,02h)` via `(A4h,58h)` to `(A5h,21h,02h)`, ending at William's cell door. The Abbot's later return to his own cell is excluded. `Abad::piensa` sets destinations 4 and 5 in that order; `Logica::iniciaPersonajes` supplies the starting position. Destination 4 stores height 0 to select the ground floor, but its terrain height is 2; the pathfinder tests the floor and x/y, not equality with that destination's height. The trace contains 201 positions, including height transitions 2–1–0–1–2. Its origin and destination portrait use the same transformation as the SVG cells. Malachi and Berengar’s scriptorium portraits use the calibrated printed-map anchors; their world positions remain in the trace data for geometry comparisons.

The disk is decoded using `DskReader` and `AbadiaDriver::reOrderAndCopy`. `Juego` advances the ROM base by 4000h: the height streams at game offsets 18A00h, 18F00h and 19080h come from bank 7, loaded by the driver at 1C000h. `tools/build-world-map.py` preserves the height cells as exact SVG rectangles/contours; hatching distinguishes blocked areas rather than inventing detailed architectural walls. Run `python tools/trace-abbot-welcome.py` to regenerate the trace, both localized SVG maps and `assets/game/abbot-welcome-route.js` (requires the sibling source checkout and the local MinGW compiler).

Validation: all 201 trace anchors avoid blocked floor cells; the largest movement is two world units, at stair transitions. A comparison with the pre-trial `week.js` confirmed identical character state and movement geometry for the other 40 phases, including the transition back to the magazine map at day I Vespers. Site-link, JavaScript and whitespace checks passed. Browser checks cover the localized maps, selection, phase changes and resizing. Decoration does not reproduce all visible game architecture: the underlying authority is the movement-height grid.

Full bilingual sweep, 2026-09-12: `tools/week-audit-2026-09-12.md` records all 41 phases, comparison with the six preserved Micromanía 33 pages and the earlier object table, and the differences between recommended collection times and actual availability. The advice follows the magazine's progression without adopting its mistaken day-II victim name or treating its centre mirror stair as universal. Scroll pickup before prime III, reading the mirror clue, lamp replacement, glove access and pursuit completion are explicit prerequisites.

- `Logica.cpp`, initialization: starts at day 1, NONA.
- `Marcador.cpp`, `avanzaMomentoDia`: COMPLETAS rolls over to NOCHE and increments the day. The night of day III precedes its prima.
- `AccionesDia.cpp`, `AccionesNoche`: night V places spectacles in the illuminated room and key I on the altar; night VI places key II on Malachi's table and activates Jorge behind the mirror.
- `AccionesDia.cpp`, `AccionesPrima`: day II removes spectacles; day III hides the book, activates Jorge near the monks' cells and relocates the scroll if William does not carry it. Day V removes key I unless William still carries it at prime; collecting and then dropping it does not prevent removal.
- Object reminders (2026-09-11): `Malaquias.cpp` collects key III from his desk at vespers and returns it when resuming his post if he holds it; Adso is its playable carrier. `AccionesPrima` makes the lamp available in the kitchen from prime III. From day III onward, `Logica::reiniciaContadoresLampara` removes/replaces a used lamp at prime even if oil remains; the traversal repeats the collection reminder at prime IV–VII. The spectacles placed on night V have no further scheduled dawn removal. `Logica::iniciaPersonajes` defines Guillermo's six item types and Adso's key III/lamp mask. These reminders appear only in the protected advice. See also `tools/game-guide-sources.md` for the object reference audit.
- `AccionesDia.cpp`, `AccionesSexta`: Bernard appears at the stairs on day IV. His announcement and physical arrival are distinct triggers.
- `Abad.cpp`, `piensa`, `frasesIglesiaEnPrima`: welcome, offices, escort, daily announcements, day V discovery of Severinus at nona, and day VII failure at terce.
- `Berengario.cpp`, `piensa`: day III night journey via the scriptorium stairs to the book, then Severinus's cell; death occurs after arrival with the book. Earlier scroll warnings depend on possession, proximity and elapsed time.
- `Severino.cpp`, `piensa`: introduction from day II at terce/nona, conditional on proximity and other dialogue; day IV terce report; day V prime warning, then waiting in his cell.
- `Malaquias.cpp`, `piensa`: library guard; vespers closure through west wing and kitchen; day V terce murder only when both characters reach Severinus's cell; day V vespers death at church. No universal sext meal route is assigned to him here: his routine can retain the library desk destination.
- `Bernardo.cpp`, `piensa`: manuscript pursuit on day IV, random destinations after delivery, departure on day V subject to office/night branches and current state.
- `Jorge.cpp`, `piensa`: day III introduction, return to shared cell at sext and deactivation; final dialogue, gloves and flight to the illuminated room.
- `Logica.cpp`, `pulsadoQR`, `compruebaAbreEspejo` and `generaNumeroRomano`: the scroll template is `SECRETUM FINIS AFRICAE, MANUS SUPRA XXX IDOLUM AGE PRIMUM ET SEPTIMUM DE QUATUOR`; the generated group replaces `XXX` and selects the mirror-room stair (IXX left, XIX centre, XXI right). Q and R, the first and seventh letters of `QUATUOR`, must be held together there. A wrong stair opens the fatal trap.
- `Adso.cpp`: following William and destinations for offices, meal and sleep.
- `GestorFrases.cpp`, Spanish and English arrays: exported by `tools/export-week-dialogue.py` with original phrase IDs.

Map: existing Retro Gamer España 41 composite already credited on the game page. Pins identify approximate rooms, not engine coordinates. Arrows connect a character's destination in the immediately preceding chronological phase to the destination in the selected phase, when both are known and different. Explicit scene journeys take precedence. These are expected routine transitions, not a claim that a save has completed every journey; player-dependent and random origins are not guessed. Stationary, dead and absent characters have no movement arrows. Jorge's conditional final flight does not imply a return to the mirror at the next prime.

All known journeys are visible, with the selected character highlighted. Portraits and both arrow endpoints use the same positioning function, including individual desk offsets, cell shifts, and the occupants of the origin phase. Church and refectory origins are the exception: their hollow ends share the exact room anchor instead of the preceding portraits' spread. Arrowheads stop just outside the destination portrait. Geometry refreshes when the map loads, is revealed, or resizes. Shared-room pins are spread around the room to remain selectable. A destination does not imply that a character is already there. Conditional deaths are shown as narrative outcomes with the trigger stated, not a claim about every possible save.

The Strings/Decoder.py utility was inspected but not used as authority: its own documentation describes incomplete lookup tables and a simplified decoding model.

Labyrinth route: the night VI and VII clues show a detail of the annotated Micromanía first-series 33 plan preserved by CPC-Power, https://www.cpc-power.com/index.php?page=detail&onglet=plan&num=222. Source image: https://www.cpc-power.com/extra_lire_fichier.php?extra=plan&fiche=222&slot=1&part=A&type=.gif (1215 × 851). `assets/maps/library-route-micromania.png` extracts the rectangle (870, 475)–(1215, 839), without redrawing or recolouring. The red annotations already occur in that archived scan; their author and date are not identified, so the public caption describes an annotated copy, not an original published solution. This is a historical route reference, independent of the explorer's NPC movement arrows. Micromanía's day-VI night walkthrough retrieves key II and the spectacles, carries the lamp, and uses the excursion to practise reaching and navigating the labyrinth; its day-VII night is the final visit to the mirror. The reconstructed logic places the spectacles on night V and leaves them available, while night VI places key II and activates Jorge, so the public chronology distinguishes engine availability from the magazine's recommended progression.
