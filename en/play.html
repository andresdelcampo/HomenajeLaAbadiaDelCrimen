Before you enter

Use a computer with a keyboard and an up-to-date browser. This player is not ready for phones or tablets.

Each selected disk or tape loads automatically. The game is in Spanish, including when you view this page in English.

The 64K edition is the CPC 464/664 version, with a reduced abbey made to fit those machines. Here it runs under the compatible CPC 6128 emulation available in RVMPlayer.

MSX tape uses black and yellow; MSX disk uses blue and yellow. Tape loading is accelerated automatically. Source.

The ZX Spectrum entries include the original 128K tape release, the MCM Software tape re-release, and MCM's Spectrum +3 disk re-release. The tape entries retain their original TZX loaders.

On Spectrum, the arrow keys are translated internally to the original A, Z, K and L keys. You can also use those four keys directly.

PC CGA offers the two preserved disk images: original and pirate. At boot, choose F1 for a colour monitor or F2 for a monochrome monitor.

Leaving the game screen pauses play. Your progress is not saved when you close or reload the page.

PCW uses Habisoft’s conversion, version 1.2, on a PCW 8256 with a green display and the DK’tronics sound expansion. Habisoft.
