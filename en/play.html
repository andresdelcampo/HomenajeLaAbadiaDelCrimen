Before you enter

Use a computer with a keyboard and an up-to-date browser. This player is not ready for phones or tablets.

The disk loads automatically. The game is in Spanish, including when you view this page in English.

The 64K edition is the CPC 464/664 version, with a reduced abbey made to fit those machines. Here it runs under the compatible CPC 6128 emulation available in RVMPlayer.

The ZX Spectrum edition is the 128K port preserved on MCM Software's Spectrum +3 disk re-release.

On Spectrum, the arrow keys are translated internally to the original A, Z, K and L keys. You can also use those four keys directly.

Leaving the game screen pauses play. Your progress is not saved when you close or reload the page.
